What to make of this

  • Scores are relative, not grades. A score of 89 on a big site doesn't mean it's well designed; our 22 automatic checks are a first pass.
  • Tap targets (24px) are flagged on almost every site, even gov.uk and GitHub, and craigslist has 776. That's a sign this check may be too strict about links in navigation and text. I recommend reviewing it.
  • Sites that block bots can't be audited from a server. Real users on real Chrome won't hit this, but the test can't cover them.
  • Brand-name titles: Google is flagged because its title is just "Google" (the check does what it says), but for well-known brands that is a judgement call worth a tolerance.
  • Pages that load content after the first view (infinite scroll, logins, pop-ups) were not seen.
