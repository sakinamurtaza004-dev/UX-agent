UX Audit Buddy: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026
Published by: Sakina Lalipurwala ("we")
Contact: sakinamurtaza004@gmail.com

UX Audit Buddy is a Chrome extension that audits the web page you are viewing for usability, accessibility and manipulative-design problems, and lets you save a report. This policy explains what data it handles.

In short

  • The audit runs on your device. Your audits, reports and settings are stored only in your browser.
  • The extension sends nothing anywhere. If you use "Ask your own AI chat", you paste a prompt into the AI website yourself.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, do not sell data, and do not load remote code.

What stays on your device

DataWhyWhere
For each audit: the page address (without its query string and #fragment), page title, the findings, scores, and notes about laws and regulationsSo you can open reports later and compare before and afterYour browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). The 50 most recent audits are kept
Your settings: history on/off and your chosen AI chatTo remember your choicesSame

You can turn history off, delete a single audit, delete all history, or export your data from the extension's History page. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

"Ask your own AI chat" (optional, no account or key)

The extension can build a ready-made prompt from your audit and copy it to your clipboard, then open the AI chat website you choose (for example ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, or an address you enter). The extension itself sends nothing: you paste the prompt into that chat yourself. The prompt contains the audit findings and, if you leave the option ticked, up to about 9,000 characters of the page's visible text (never text you typed into form fields). You can read the exact text in the preview before copying. What the chat service does with what you paste is governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy, and we do not receive it.

Pictures in your downloaded report

When you click Download report on a page you just audited, the extension takes screenshots of the problem areas on that page (using the access your toolbar click gave it) and puts them in the PDF file that is saved to your computer. The pictures are not stored by the extension and are not sent anywhere. They can show whatever is on the page, so check the PDF before you share it, especially for pages with private information.

Highlighting problems on the page

When you click Show on page, the extension draws outlines over the affected elements of the page you are viewing. This happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the outlines disappear when you press Esc, click Clear, or reload the page.

Fonts and other resources

The extension's font and icons are included in the extension itself. It does not contact any font, analytics or advertising server. Reports (PDF, HTML, JSON) are created on your device and saved through your browser's normal download; nothing is uploaded.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for advertising, and no use of it for purposes unrelated to producing your audit and review.
  • No remotely hosted code: all of the extension's code ships inside the extension.
  • No reading of any page unless you open the extension on it and ask for a scan (the extension asks Chrome only for the permissions listed below).

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices and rights

Because audits are stored on your device, you control them directly (delete, export, turn history off). If you are in the EU/UK, India or another region with privacy laws, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you and to have it deleted. This extension stores everything on your own device, so you can delete it yourself, and you can contact us at the email above with any questions.

Changes

If we change what the extension collects or shares, we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect.
